02 — How it works

Four moves, on repeat.

A rollage is an old paper-craft trick: cut a picture into strips and weave it back together. This page just does it faster.

  1. 01

    Mirror

    The picture is mirrored into four panels, like folding paper before cutting a snowflake, so every edge matches its neighbour.

  2. 02

    Slice

    The mirrored base is cut into Cuts vertical strips.

  3. 03

    Shuffle

    Strips are re-ordered from the ends inward: 1 8 2 7 3 6 4 5.

  4. 04

    Turn & repeat

    The same trick runs on the rows, then the whole thing repeats Steps times.
